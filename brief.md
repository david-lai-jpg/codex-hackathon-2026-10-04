# DevDay Exchange Community Hack Day: Taipei

hackathon event url: https://luma.com/q31z8elq

Sunday 2026-10-04, 12:00-17:00 Asia/Taipei (GMT+8), OOPS創潮玩圈, 台北市松山區敦化北路100號15樓.
Unofficial community hack day. Build with Codex and OpenAI tools. Teams of 1-4.

deadline: 2026-10-04T17:00:00+08:00

## Tracks (optional topic prompts; your own idea is also allowed)

- track: Fix an everyday frustration (remove a repetitive task at work, school, or home)
- track: Build for Taipei (transport, food, events, accessibility, or community life)
- track: Turn information into action (documents, meeting notes, or research into something useful)
- track: Make learning personal (interactive tutor, practice partner, or learning game)
- track: Build for someone else (real problem for a small business, nonprofit, or community)
- track: Surprise us (playful or unexpected use of AI)

## Judging

- criterion: Usefulness
- criterion: Creativity
- criterion: Convincing working demo

Peer vote after the final demo: each checked-in attendee ranks their top 3 teams, excluding their own (3/2/1 points). Voting closes 10 minutes after the final demo. Ties: first-choice votes, then a judging panel.

## Submission

Rules given on the day (2026-10-04):

1. Submit the code by 14:30.
2. Then submit a 1-minute YouTube video.
3. If the video is chosen, the team gives a 1-minute live demo: the problem, what you built, and how
   it works.

## Prizes

Top 3 teams: 1 year of ChatGPT Pro per approved team member, plus API-credit prizes.
Eligible checked-in attendees: $100 Codex credits and $50 API credits.
